# Ferreira Armond — FAN references (Muriaé/Leopoldina probate records)

FamilySearch **Full-Text** hits where **Simplício José Ferreira Armond** or
**Aristão Ferreira Armond** appear as **parties, witnesses, creditors,
appraisers (louvados) or attorneys in THIRD-PARTY probate/court records** of the
**Muriaé and Leopoldina comarcas** (Zona da Mata, Minas Gerais). These are
**FAN references** (friends/associates/neighbours) — retained for the family's
timeline, places, standing (Simplício = "Capitão"; a fazenda condômino) and
network. They are **not** individually catalogued as sources.

The two **subject** records from the same Full-Text batch (about the family
itself) were catalogued and moved to `evidence/probate/`:
- **SRC-0017** — 1881 Leopoldina inventário heir list (Eliza Balbina de Toledo +
  her Toledo siblings).
- **SRC-0018** — 1884 Leopoldina petition of Simplício, heir of D. Mathilde
  Maria de Jezus.

The 1973 driver registry (ARK `3:1:3QHV-L7VF-WWJN`) was a **duplicate of
SRC-0008** and was removed. The full candidate list (including non-downloaded and
false-positive rows) is in `fulltext_candidates.csv`.
