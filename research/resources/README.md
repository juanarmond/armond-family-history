# Research resource references

Retained third-party reference documents (published academic articles, census
and parish transcriptions) kept as private research **leads**, not evidence.
Each is a copy of a public source; its original URL and access date are recorded
so the lead stays reproducible. Nothing here is a source-qualified conclusion.
Material about historical families that merely share a surname is **context
only** — never proof of descent for this repository's line.

## Files

### `asbrap_rev6_art10.pdf` — "Armond, Por Quê?" (Rui Vieira da Cunha)

- Source: `https://www.asbrap.org.br/artigos/rev6_art10.pdf` — Revista da ASBRAP
  nº 6, pp. 223-228. Retrieved 2026-07-29. (The PDF's embedded Title/Author
  metadata is a wrong template artifact; the article is the one named here.)
- What it is: a genealogical article establishing that the **Armond** surname of
  the Barbacena (Minas Gerais) family is **Azorean, not French**, and tracing the
  name's etymology — a Scottish surname carried to Madeira then Ilha Terceira,
  written variably Drummond / Ormonde / Ermonde / Armonde. It refutes the Eduardo
  de Meneses (1926) "French from Madeira" story, rests on cited Azorean parish
  certificates, and is corroborated by the genealogists J. C. Soares Ferreira and
  Jorge Forjaz / Jorge Pamplona Ferraz (Angra do Heroísmo).
- CAVEAT: this concerns the **historical Barbacena Ferreira Armond family**,
  which is **not** linked to Aristão Ferreira Armond (this repository's line). It
  is a context lead for the surname's deep origin, usable only if the
  Piacatuba→Barbacena bridge is ever proven. See
  `../logs/2026-07-29-ferreira-armond-deep-dig-and-locality-catalogue.md`.
- Key names / leads (all Ilha Terceira, freguesia de São Sebastião, Açores,
  unless noted) — a ready pedigree for the GEA parish images if the bridge holds:
  - **Francisco Ferreira Armond** — b. 1691-1694 São Sebastião; d. 16 Nov 1751
    Barbacena (testament 9 Nov 1751); the Brazilian patriarch. Recorded once as
    "Ferreira do Souto" (1721). m. 25 Jan 1721 (Igreja Matriz, São Sebastião)
    **Ângela Maria da Conceição Camelo** (bapt. 29 Apr 1700; d. 2 Apr 1746
    Barbacena).
  - Ângela's parents: **Bento Faleiro** and **Francisca Nunes** (both d. 16 May
    1722, São Sebastião).
  - Francisco's parents: **Gaspar de Souto Maior** (bapt. 23 Dec 1657; d. 2 Dec
    1727) and **Margarida das Candeias Machado** (bapt. 29 May 1667; d. 9 Apr
    1693; m. 2 Oct 1690, Igreja N. Sra. da Graça, São Sebastião).
  - Gaspar's parents: Alferes **Belchior da Ponte Cardoso** and **Mariana
    Fagundes** (his 2nd wife).
  - Margarida's parents: **Francisco Ferreira de Ermonde** (a.k.a. Ferreira
    Drummond / Ferreira de Andrade) and **Ana Machado**.
  - Francisco Ferreira de Ermonde's parents: **Sebastião Gato Ferreira** (a.k.a.
    Drummond / Machado) and **Maria de Andrade**; he was paternal grandson (via
    the 1st marriage, to **Bárbara Gato**) of **Francisco Ferreira Drummond**,
    madeirense — the trunk of the line on Terceira.

### `projetocompartilhar-1831-curral-novo-censo.pdf` — 1831 census, Curral Novo (Barbacena, MG)

- Source: `http://www.projetocompartilhar.org/censos/1831CurralNovoMG.pdf`
  (Projeto Compartilhar; a transcription — the manuscript original is at the
  Arquivo Público Mineiro). Retrieved 2026-07-29.
- What it is: the 1831 household census (fogos) of the district of N. Sra. do
  Rosário do Curral Novo, Termo de Barbacena. **Fogo 14** is the Ferreira
  Armonde household (widow **Felizarda Maria Francisca de Assis**, 70, with her
  unmarried adult children incl. **Simplício José Ferreira, 46**; 66 cativos),
  and the census is signed by "**Simplício José Ferreira Armond, Juiz de Paz**".
  Primary-source support for the anti-merge (the Gen-2 Simplício was 46 and
  single in 1831 → not Marfiza's/Aristão's father). Also records professions and
  slave-holdings = wealth signals.
- Caveat: transcription (verify against the APM manuscript for anything
  critical); concerns the historical Barbacena family (context lead).

### `lacerda-2010-ferreira-armonde-uff-thesis.pdf` — Lacerda 2010 (UFF doctoral thesis)

- Source: `https://www.historia.uff.br/stricto/teses/Tese-2010_Antonio_Henrique_Lacerda-S.pdf`.
  Retrieved 2026-07-29.
- What it is: "Negócios de Minas: família, fortuna, poder e redes de
  sociabilidades nas Minas Gerais — a família Ferreira Armonde (1751-1850)", by
  Antônio Henrique Duarte Lacerda. The primary, document-based study (inventories
  post-mortem, the 1831 census) behind the anti-merge (Simplício b.1784 died
  solteiro) and the family's wealth (1845 monte-mor ~R$ 321:751$000). Context
  lead; not this line's proven ancestry.

### `senra-ferreira-armond-blog-snapshot.txt` — Mauro Senra, "Ferreira Armond — Uma Família Mineira"

  Text snapshot retrieved 2026-07-29 (kept because the blog is a live webpage

### `projetocompartilhar-1831-joao-gomes-censo.pdf` — 1831 census, João Gomes (Barbacena, MG)

- Source: `http://www.projetocompartilhar.org/Censos/1831JoaoGomesMG.pdf`
  (Projeto Compartilhar; APM MP Cx 7 Doc 1). Retrieved 2026-07-29.
- What it is: the 1831 census of the Distrito de São Miguel e Almas de João
  Gomes (Freguesia do Engenho do Mato), Termo de Barbacena. **Fogo 22 names a
  MARRIED Armond: Manoel Antonio de Armond (30, casado, tropeiro), wife Barbara
  Maria (22), son Camillo Antonio (7), 2 cativos** — unlike the celibate Curral
  Novo brothers, a married Armond branch near Barbacena, and thus a candidate
  source for a *later* "Simplício" namesake. Lead only; no proven link.

### `projetocompartilhar-1751-francisco-ferreira-armonde-inventario.htm` — 1751 will/inventory

- Source: `http://www.projetocompartilhar.org/DocsMgAF/FranciscoFerreiraArmond1751.htm`
  (transcription; IPHAN São João del Rei inventory + Barbacena óbitos microfilm
  1252370). Retrieved 2026-07-29.
- What it is: the will/inventory of Francisco Ferreira Armonde (d. 16 Nov 1751,
  Rio Fundo, Borda do Campo/Barbacena), declaring himself "natural da Ilha
  Terceira, freguesia da Vila de São Sebastião, filho de Gaspar do Souto Maior e
  Margarida das Candeias" — corroborates the Azorean origin from the will itself.
  Wealth ~1:480$000 + 119 oitavas de ouro + ≥4 slaves; illiterate. Context lead.

## Target resources (identified, not retrievable by this assistant — browser/human)

- **Forjaz & Ornelas Mendes, "Genealogias da Ilha Terceira" (vols 1–9)** — the
  authoritative genealogy of Terceira families; on Scribd (user
  `Joao-Henrique-F-Vieira`) but Scribd is bot-walled (a "Client Challenge"
  interstitial). It is a secondary compilation; the correct *evidence* route is
  the primary parish registers of São Sebastião / Angra do Heroísmo (1541–1911),
  free at the GEA (`culturacores.azores.gov.pt/ig/registos`). Both need a browser.
- **My Portuguese Gen** (`myportuguesegen.blogspot.com`) — a curated directory of
  Portugal/Azores archives (DigitArq/Tombo, GEA registos + passaportes, BPARLSR
  "fontes genealógicas", CEPESE, BN Digital, UMinho GHP). A search map for the
  Azorean leads, of use once the Piacatuba→Barbacena bridge is proven.
