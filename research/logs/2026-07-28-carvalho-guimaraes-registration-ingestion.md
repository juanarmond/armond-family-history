# Carvalho Guimarães collective birth-registration ingestion

## Research question

What original record supports the inherited claim that Vicente José de
Carvalho Guimarães was Portuguese, and does it identify his Portuguese place
of origin?

## Source path

Maria Aurora Guimarães's FamilySearch profile `GQVR-167` has a patron-created
source titled `Certidao de nascimento`, attachment `7XVH-XDK`. The source links
to artifact `217929351`, uploaded as `certidao de nascimento.jpeg`.

The artifact is a 2025 full-content certificate headed for João José de
Carvalho Guimarães. It transcribes one 1915 civil registration covering six
siblings, including the direct ancestor named in the entry as **Maria Amora
Guimarães**.

The authorised FamilySearch Download action supplied the originally uploaded
768×1040 JPEG. That file is retained as `CIV-0007`; no thumbnail, screenshot,
crop, reconstruction or upscaled derivative is used.

## Material statements

The registration was made on 12 October 1915 in São Francisco do Glória,
municipality and judicial district of Carangola. Francisco José de Carvalho
Guimarães retrospectively registered six children born at Fazenda do Barroso:

- José de Carvalho Guimarães, 8 November 1901;
- Maria Amora Guimarães, 1 January 1904;
- Maria da Conceição Guimarães, 8 September 1906;
- Sebastião José de Carvalho Guimarães, 2 June 1909;
- João José de Carvalho Guimarães, 16 December 1912; and
- Maria de Lourdes Guimarães, 24 June 1915.

The source describes them as legitimate children of Francisco and his wife
Emmerenciana Maria de Jesus. It reports:

- Francisco's parents as Vicente José de Carvalho Guimarães and Maria
  Tertuliana da Conceição;
- Emmerenciana's parents as Antonio Francisco da Silva and Maria Paula de
  Jesus;
- Francisco and Maria Tertuliana as natives of Vila do Rio Claro;
- Emmerenciana, Antonio and Maria Paula as natives of São Francisco do Glória;
- Vicente as `portuguez` and deceased in Carangola;
- Maria Tertuliana as deceased in Carangola;
- Antonio as deceased in São Francisco do Glória; and
- Maria Paula as resident in São Francisco do Glória in 1915.

The certificate supplies nationality only for Vicente. It does not identify a
Portuguese district, municipality, island or parish.

## Name conflict

The closest available birth evidence says **Maria Amora Guimarães**. Later
family information and the collaborative profile use **Maria Aurora
Guimarães**. The structured preferred name now follows the 1915 registration,
while the later form remains preserved as a separate variant. The conflict
must be revisited when her own 1991 death registration is ingested.

## Search beyond the profiles

Exact and spouse-linked FamilySearch historical-record searches produced no
qualifying marriage or death record for Vicente. Exact full-text searching did
find a power of attorney dated 27 November 1866 in Vila do Rio Claro naming a
resident **Vicente José de Carvalho Guimarães**:

- image ARK `3:1:3Q9M-C373-33NR-G`;
- image group `105938261`;
- image 1404 in the current viewer;
- volume `Fundo 10094_Caixa 16831524_Maco 3743`; and
- creator `Arquivo Central do TJRJ`, with the displayed custodians Arquivo
  Público do Estado do Rio de Janeiro and Fundo de Presidência Provincial do
  Rio de Janeiro.

The exact name and Vila do Rio Claro location make this a relevant lead, but
the document names no spouse, child or parent. Identity with `P-0023` is not
established, so it has not been catalogued as evidence for this family.

## Structured ingestion

`CIV-0007` supports:

- six new direct-ancestor person entities;
- three family groups, without inventing partnerships for reported
  co-parents;
- the exact 1904 birth event for Maria Amora;
- source-reported birthplace, death-place and residence events for the older
  direct ancestors; and
- three historically worded place entities.

All conclusions remain `strong-evidence` because the retained artifact is a
modern certified derivative and the underlying 1915 register image has not
been inspected.

## Next action

Test the 1866 power-of-attorney identity against Francisco's chronology and
search the Rio Claro and Carangola marriage and death series for a record that
links Vicente to Maria Tertuliana or Francisco. Only after a qualifying record
states a Portuguese locality should the search move to a Portuguese archive or
conservatory.
