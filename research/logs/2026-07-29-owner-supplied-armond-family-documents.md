# Owner-supplied Armond-family documents: Aristão's siblings and the Eugenópolis locus

## Research question

Assess the documents the owner located (via FamilySearch Full-Text and a
newspaper) about Aristão Ferreira Armond's family, and refine the search.

## What is now strongly established

- **Marfiza is Aristão's sister.** Two independent primary civil records — his
  1957 death and her 1962 death — name the **same parents**, Simplicio Ferreira
  Armond and Eliza/Elisa (Ferreira) Toledo. Her age (89 in 1962 → b. ~1873)
  matches the 15 Feb 1873 Piacatuba baptism.
- **Elisa's maiden surname is Toledo** — Marfiza's death gives the mother as
  "Eliza Ferreira **Toledo**", corroborating in a primary record the "Elisa
  Balbina Tolledo" previously known only from the Senra blog.
- **Simplício and Elisa were married** — "filho/filha legítima" in two civil
  records (Aristão, Marfiza) is strong evidence of the marriage, even without the
  marriage act.

## The key geographic redirect

José Olavo was **natural de Eugenópolis (MG)**. Combined with the family's other
records — Geraldo × Cidalia married in **Eugenópolis** (SRC-0002); Antenor × Iris
married in **Alvorada** (a district of Eugenópolis, SRC-0005); Cidalia and Iris
born in Alvorada — the family of Aristão × Liliosa was **based in Eugenópolis,
Zona da Mata, MG**, not Leopoldina.

Consequence: **search the Aristão × Liliosa marriage, and the births of Geraldo
and José Olavo, in Eugenópolis and its districts (Alvorada; possibly "Limeira"),
not in Leopoldina.** Aristão's own baptism (~1879) stays in the Piacatuba/
Leopoldina area (his birth family). "Limeira" (Geraldo's 1915 birthplace, and a
reported but unverified "Aristão subdelegado de polícia de Limeira, 1931") is
most likely a locality of the Eugenópolis/Carangola micro-region of MG — to be
confirmed; not assumed to be São Paulo.

## What this does NOT prove

It does **not** prove the Barbacena/Azorean bridge. Simplício's own parents
remain unknown, and no record links this Simplício to the elite Barbacena
Ferreira Armonde family. The Azorean origin stays an unproven hypothesis; only
the immediate family (Aristão's parents and a sister) is now solid.
