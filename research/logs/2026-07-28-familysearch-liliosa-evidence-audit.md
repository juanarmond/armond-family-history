## Attached Source

The sole source was `SJBH-LL3`, titled:

> Liliosa Paz Armond Armond, “Brasil, Rio de Janeiro, Registro Civil,
> 1804-2013”

Its linked person-record ARK was `1:1:77DD-TX2M`. Inspection showed that this is
not Liliosa's death registration. It is a person-level extraction for Liliosa
from the 1991 death registration of her son, Geraldo Paz Armond. It resolves to
the same register page and image ARK `3:1:S3HT-DYSS-YKV` already catalogued as
`CIV-0003`.

The record supports only the proposition that Geraldo's death informant
reported Liliosa as his mother. It cannot support Liliosa's own date of death,
place of death, age, original surname or parents. No new source identifier was
allocated.

- upload filename: `per829587_1975_00042.pdf`;
- contributor: JuanArmond;
- contribution date: 7 February 2021;
- visibility observed: Public;
- tagged profiles: Aristão Ferreira Armond (`GW4P-R9Z`), Liliosa Paz Armond

The eight-page PDF is a scanned issue of the newspaper *O Processo*, issue 42,
dated 1-15 February 1975 on its front page. It is not a 1946 civil registration
or parish burial record.

The PDF was downloaded to a temporary review directory, its text layer was
searched, and all eight pages were rendered and inspected. No identifiable
occurrence of Liliosa, Aristão or the surname Armond was found. Poor OCR means
this is not proof that the issue contains no indirect family reference, but it
is sufficient to reject the PDF as Liliosa's death evidence. The temporary PDF,
text and page renders were deleted after review. No source or inventory record
was created.
