# Espírito Santo marriage-book access review

## Research question

Can the completed marriage ceremony entry for João Monis Bittencourt and
Susanna Rita Brondão be located online after the provision issued on
23 December 1882?

## Searches completed

The authenticated FamilySearch historical-record index was searched for the
couple in 1882–1883 using exact and broader combinations of:

- João, Joao, Monis, Muniz and Bittencourt;
- Susanna, Susana, Rita, Brondão and Brandão; and
- Rio de Janeiro as the marriage place.

The exact search returned no result. The broader search returned the already
catalogued provision, `PAR-0001`, as its only result for the target couple. No
separate ceremony entry was exposed.

The FamilySearch catalog record for *Brazil, Rio de Janeiro, Catholic Church
records, 1616–1980* was then reviewed in the authenticated session. Its current
film-collection list includes Santíssimo Sacramento and other Rio de Janeiro
parishes, but it does not expose a separate Espírito Santo parish series. A
keyword-only image search was too broad to establish absence and is not treated
as exhaustive.

## Historical and custodial findings

The Archdiocese of Rio de Janeiro's historical-preservation commission states
that:

- the Espírito Santo parish was founded on 8 July 1865;
- the Espírito Santo chapel served as the parish seat until 1908; and
- the later parish seat was transferred to the church now called São Joaquim.

The chapel was therefore the relevant parish seat in December 1882. This
supports the place named in the provision but does not prove that the ceremony
occurred.

The provision image identifies the record custodian as the **Arquivo da Cúria
Metropolitana do Rio de Janeiro**. The absence of a separately exposed
FamilySearch film series is an online-access gap, not evidence that the original
book or entry was never created.

## Required archival request

With the owner's explicit authorisation, request the following from the Arquivo
da Cúria Metropolitana do Rio de Janeiro:

> Please search the marriage register of the Paróquia do Espírito Santo for the
> marriage of João Monis Bittencourt and Susanna Rita Brondão after
> 23 December 1882, initially through 31 March 1883. The supporting provision
> was issued on 23 December 1882 and is recorded in the Santíssimo Sacramento
> provision register, DGS 004626365, item 1, image 21, printed page 191. Please
> provide the archival reference and a full-resolution digital image or
> certified copy if an entry is found, and report the searched book and date
> span if it is not found.

Search variants should include `João/Joao`, `Monis/Muniz`,
`Susanna/Susana`, and `Brondão/Brandão`.

No archival enquiry was sent during this review.

## Conclusion

The completed ceremony entry was not located online. The record remains
`inaccessible`: a specific likely custodian and request target are known, but
the relevant marriage book is not separately exposed in the reviewed
FamilySearch catalog.

The 23 December 1882 date remains the provision date only. It must not be
promoted to a completed marriage date without the ceremony entry or equivalent
primary evidence.
