# FamilySearch living-profile download and academic-article review

## Research question

Which original files attached to the living repository subject's FamilySearch
Memories can be retained without duplication, what primary genealogical
evidence do the civil records contain, and does the attached academic
dissertation add any defensible knowledge about the documented Armond line?

## Authorisation and method

The repository owner explicitly authorised downloading the files into this
private repository and stated that duplicate documents should not be retained.
The authenticated FamilySearch Memories page for profile `GQJ1-N2D` was
reviewed without changing any profile, tag, source or visibility setting.

FamilySearch's `Download` action supplied each originally uploaded PDF. Every
file was checked for byte size, page count and SHA-256 checksum. The civil
pages were rendered for visual review while the repository copies remained
byte-for-byte unchanged. The 185-page dissertation was read through its text
layer, with the Ferreira Armond section and cited references reviewed
separately.

## Civil-record evidence

`SRC-0011` directly reports the repository subject's birth and parentage and
names both sets of grandparents. It corroborates the existing working roster
without resolving any disputed deceased-ancestor fact.

`SRC-0012` directly records the legal marriage in Toronto on 16 August 2003 and
reports the parties' parentage. `SRC-0013`, `SRC-0014` and `SRC-0015` are later
Italian civil-registry manifestations of that same event. They corroborate the
marriage but are not independent original-event observations.

No full transcription of sensitive living-person fields is included in the
research log. The private source records contain only the minimum abstract
needed for genealogy.

## Academic dissertation review

The attached work is Gabriel Afonso Vieira Chagas, *Estratégia de família:
casamentos endogâmicos em grupos familiares do entorno da Serra do Camapuã
(1750-1890)*, a 2018 UFMG master's dissertation.

Its Ferreira Armond section, on PDF pages 153-156, provides useful historical
context:

- it describes an historical Ferreira Armond group active in São João del-Rei,
  Barbacena, the Paraibuna region and the Zona da Mata;
- it states that this group was of Azorean origin, relying on earlier secondary
  research rather than presenting a source chain to the repository's family;
- it describes two marriages on 12 June 1804 at the chapel of Nossa Senhora do
  Rosário do Curral Novo, in the parish of Nossa Senhora da Piedade de
  Barbacena, which connected Ferreira Armond and Ferreira da Fonseca siblings;
  and
- it repeatedly cites Antônio Henrique Duarte Lacerda, *Negócios de Minas:
  família, fortuna, poder e redes de sociabilidades nas Minas Gerais - a
  família Ferreira Armonde (1751-1850)*, UFF doctoral thesis, 2010.

The dissertation does not name Aristão Ferreira Armond, Simplicio Armand or
Liliosa Paz Armond and provides no chain from them to the historical group.
The Azorean-origin statement therefore remains a research lead, not a
conclusion about this repository's documented Armond line.

## Negative findings and limitations

- No document in this set resolves Aristão's birth, baptism or marriage.
- The dissertation contains no person-specific bridge to Aristão or his
  source-reported parents.
- The Italian records derive from the Canadian marriage and must not be counted
  as four independent confirmations.
- No attempt was made to publish, redistribute or change the visibility of any
  FamilySearch Memory.

## Conclusion

Six unique original PDFs were retained and catalogued as `SRC-0011` through
`SRC-0016`. One duplicated birth upload was reduced to a single preservation
file without losing either Memory's provenance. The civil records provide
private evidence about living people. The dissertation adds a useful secondary
lead to Lacerda's work and the 1804 Barbacena marriages, but it does not prove
an Azorean origin or historical Ferreira Armond descent for this family.
