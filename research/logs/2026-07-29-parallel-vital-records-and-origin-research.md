# Parallel read-only research: Liliosa, the Ferreira Armond bridge, Vicente and Sapucaia

## Research questions

Four independent read-only web-research threads, run in parallel while
authorised FamilySearch retrieval waits on the external Codex agent:

1. Public routes to Liliosa Paz Armond's 1946 death, her marriage to Aristão
   Ferreira Armond, and her ~1885 birth.
2. The documented family of Simplício [José] Ferreira Armond × Elisa Balbina
   Toledo (Aristão's reported parents), and whether it links to the Azorean
   Barbacena "Ferreira Armonde" family.
3. Vicente José de Carvalho Guimarães's Portuguese origin, bridge-record-first.
4. The archive and access route for the 1882–83 Santo Antônio de Sapucaia
   marriage of João Monis Bittencourt × Susanna Rita Brondão.

## Method and access

Four parallel agents using public web only (WebFetch/WebSearch); no
authenticated FamilySearch session. All findings are navigation leads, never
evidence; no zero-result index proves absence from an unindexed register.

Access test (empirical): the official archive search systems are unreadable by
the assistant's fetch tool and require the Codex browser agent or a person:
`digitarq.arquivos.pt` (Torre do Tombo) and `arquivos.azores.gov.pt` render
results via JavaScript (Archeevo); Arquivo Nacional "Entrada de Estrangeiros"
(Rio) is CAPTCHA-walled; CEPESE and the APM immigrant-hostel DB are POST forms;
FamilySearch catalog/images and Blogger post bodies are login/JS-walled.

## Thread 1 — Liliosa Paz Armond (P-0009)

- **Death — reframed to Barra Mansa.** In 1946 Volta Redonda was a district of
  Barra Mansa (emancipated 1954) and its own óbito sub-registry appears to have
  opened only ~November 1946 (CSN steelworks era). A death dated 16 April 1946
  (and plausibly the 15 November lead) most likely sits in the **Barra Mansa**
  death index/books, which are unsearched. FamilySearch inventory: Barra Mansa
  óbitos 1889–2005, death index 1889–1982 (catalog 516378); Volta Redonda
  óbitos from 1894 (so a pre-November-1946 VR image group should also exist,
  distinct from DGS 004366685 which begins November 1946). Registry: Cartório
  Souza Reis, 1º Distrito, Rua Duque de Caxias 391, Centro, Barra Mansa/RJ,
  tel (24) 3323-3228.
- **Marriage — most likely Piacatuba/Leopoldina, MG**, not Barra Mansa (the
  1946 RJ death reflects CSN-era migration, not Liliosa's origin). Window
  ~1902–1918 (bounded only by her ~1885 birth). Targets: Paróquia N. Sra. da
  Piedade de Piacatuba; Paróquia São Sebastião de Leopoldina (paroquiais
  1852–1924); Minas Gerais civil registration, Leopoldina 1879–1949. The
  marriage record is the highest value: it names her parents and origin.
- **"Paz" inference (flagged, not asserted):** the sons carry "Paz" (Geraldo
  Paz Armond), so "Paz"/"da Paz" is plausibly Liliosa's maiden/family surname;
  search indices under Paz as well as Armond and the given name Liliosa.
- **Negative / dead-ends:** registrocivil.org.br free locator covers only SP and
  PE (RJ excluded); TJRJ CNO only from 2007; Arquivo Nacional/SIAN "registro
  civil da cidade do Rio" covers only the former Distrito Federal, not Barra
  Mansa. Open-web name searches for Aristão, Geraldo, José Olavo and Liliosa
  returned nothing qualifying; the sons' birth years could not be fixed online.

## Thread 2 — Ferreira Armond bridge (P-0016 Simplício, P-0017 Elisa; Aristão P-0008)

- **Publicly documented:** Simplício **José** Ferreira Armond × Elisa Balbina
  **Tolledo** → daughter **Marfiza** (bapt. 15 Feb 1873, N. Sra. da Piedade,
  Piacatuba). The register transcription gives the father's fuller name with
  the middle name *José*. Recorded surname variants: Armond / Armonde /
  Drummond; Toledo / Tolledo. Source: cantoni.pro.br "Primeiros batismos em
  Piacatuba" (transcription of the register, 1851–1880) and the Mauro Senra
  blog (compiled genealogy).
- **Bounded negative:** the Piacatuba baptism transcription spans 1851–1880 and
  contains **exactly one** Armond child (Marfiza, 1873). Aristão is absent from
  it and from every public source checked (blog, Lacerda, Chagas). Consistent
  with Aristão being baptised **after 1880** or in **another parish** (Além
  Paraíba), not proof of absence from the underlying register.
- **Barbacena Azorean tree (documented, for contrast — not this couple's proven
  ancestry):** patriarch Francisco Ferreira Armond(e) × Ângela Maria da
  Conceição (m. Angra do Heroísmo, 27 Nov 1721), Fazenda dos Moinhos, freg. da
  Piedade de Barbacena. **The patriarch's own origin is itself contested in the
  sources** — Ilha Terceira / freg. São Sebastião (thesis) vs. a French/Madeira
  descent claim (blog). Do not assert either.
- **Anti-merge (doubly documented):** the founding-generation **Simplício José
  Ferreira Armonde, b. 1784, died unmarried** (Lacerda, from the 1845 inventory
  and the 1831 Curral Novo census, "solteiro", Juiz de Paz; the blog adds he
  died by suicide, unmarried, and was a godparent in 1821). He cannot be
  Marfiza's/Aristão's father; the Piacatuba Simplício is a later namesake.
- **Bridge verdict: plausible but unproven** — geographically credible (Armond
  members migrated into the Zona da Mata / Além Paraíba, adjacent to Piacatuba)
  but no record links the Piacatuba Simplício to the Barbacena tree, and no
  source gives his parents. Chagas states the exact caution our rules require:
  a shared surname only *suggests* kinship and needs other sources.
- **Correction to an earlier baseline:** only the Chagas **2018** UFMG
  dissertation was located (§4.6 "A família Ferreira Armond"); a "Chagas 2024"
  work was not found and should not be cited until verified.

## Thread 3 — Vicente José de Carvalho Guimarães (P-0023)

- **Parish structure fixed:** Carangola's parish is **Santa Luzia do Carangola**
  (began as the Curato de Santa Luzia, subsidiary of the **Freguesia de
  Tombos**). Santa Luzia has filmed marriages 1898–1924 and baptisms
  1871–1873 + 1891–1903. A marriage predating 1898 would be at the mother
  parish, **Tombos**.
- **Origin-bearing targets:** FamilySearch collection 2177275 — Santa Luzia
  marriages 1898–1924 and any habilitação (names naturalidade + parents), and
  the Tombos marriages for a pre-1898 union; FamilySearch civil collection
  3479702 — Carangola 1879–1949, for Vicente's óbito (before 12 Oct 1915), which
  typically states naturalidade, parents and the surviving widow.
- **Name-searchable browser databases (no locality required):** CEPESE passport
  DB (`remessas.cepese.pt`, ~1835–1911, weighted to northern Portugal), Arquivo
  Nacional Rio "Entrada de Estrangeiros" 1875–1910 (`bases.an.gov.br`, CAPTCHA),
  APM immigrant-hostel SIAAPM. None searched (POST/CAPTCHA); queue for Codex.
- **Negatives:** APM AtoM catalog, the Carangola transcription blog feed, and
  open web returned no hit for Vicente or Maria Tertuliana. Near-miss FS tree
  person "Tertuliana Maria da Conceição (1871–1971)" — reversed name, late death,
  probably unrelated. Catalog 382683 "Registros paroquiais 1804–1954" likely not
  Carangola (1804 start) — verify when logged in. TJMG Carangola inventário is
  probably physical (contact TJMG Memória do Judiciário).

## Thread 4 — João Monis Bittencourt × Susanna Rita Brondão, Sapucaia (P-0019, P-0020)

- **Two records, two custodies (key refinement):**
  1. The **1882 provision + matrimonial habilitação (banhos)** → Arquivo da
     Cúria Metropolitana do Rio de Janeiro (ACMRJ). SRC-0009 is DGS 004626365,
     item 1, image 21/35, page 191, filmed from the **Sé / Santíssimo Sacramento
     provisões volume** — not a Sapucaia parish book. A fuller habilitação
     process (naming both spouses' parents, ages, naturalidade, witnesses)
     plausibly sits at ACMRJ; request via the paid form at
     `catedral.com.br/arquivo` (presencial R$50/h, online R$70/h, certidão
     R$100).
  2. The **completed ceremony assento** (livro de casamentos, Santo Antônio de
     Sapucaia, 1882–83) → the **Paróquia Santo Antônio de Sapucaia** (Praça
     Barão de Ayuruoca 80) first, then the **Diocese/Cúria de Valença** (created
     27 Mar 1925). **Ruled out:** the Cúria de Niterói (ASBRAP holdings survey
     lists its parishes; Sapucaia is not among them) and the Cúria do Rio for
     the completed assento.
- **FamilySearch:** DGS 004626365 is online (SRC-0009 already held). Candidate
  catalog 145484 "Registros paroquiais 1880-1971" — parish unconfirmed, check
  logged in. FamilySearch coverage of Sapucaia's own casamentos register is not
  demonstrated (the provision was filmed from the Sé volume).
- **Collaborative-tree leads (login-walled, not evidence, flagged):** João Muniz
  Bittencourt `GLMF-KCR` (b. ~1864, origin "São Miguel", an 1871 "naturalisation"
  Hemeroteca link) and Suzana Ritta Brandão `LB1N-YC9`. A Brazilian
  naturalisation implies a foreign-born (Portuguese) applicant, so this
  **supports**, not contradicts, the São Miguel (Azores) origin lead. The only
  tension is the date: an individual naturalisation in 1871 (before the 1889
  "Grande Naturalização") implies an adult applicant, so the collaborative
  ~1864 birth is probably too late rather than the naturalisation being wrong.
  Two origin-bearing records to pull: (i) the 1871 naturalisation act itself
  (Hemeroteca / official gazette), which may state his naturalidade, and (ii)
  the marriage assento/habilitação naturalidade field. Pursue São Miguel in the
  GEA parish images only once a freguesia is fixed.
- **Baptism logic (no island assumption):** the Santo Antônio de Sapucaia parish
  existed only from 1871, so adults marrying in 1882–83 were baptised elsewhere;
  if locally born, target N. Sra. da Aparecida de Sapucaia (freguesia 1842) and
  neighbours (Paraíba do Sul, Sumidouro, Carmo, São José do Vale do Rio Preto,
  the Além Paraíba border). The naturalidade in the assento/habilitação is the
  prerequisite; pursue São Miguel/Azores (GEA) only if a record states an island.
- **Also:** the Colégio Brasileiro de Genealogia "Fichas Matrimoniais do Rio de
  Janeiro" (115k+ RJ marriages indexed from parish books; members-only) could
  hold the Sapucaia 1883 marriage card (parish + book/page).

## Portugal / Azores workflow (from the three owner-supplied links)

- **Do not pay for certidões for our people.** Vicente (and any island-origin
  João) predate 1911 and are 100+ years old, so their parish records sit in the
  regional **Arquivo Distrital** and are largely free and digitised on
  **DigitArq**. The paid routes — `civilonline.mj.pt`/IRN (~€10, post-1911) and
  ANTT via `crav.arquivos.pt` — are for post-1911 or non-digitised records.
- **Torre do Tombo (ANTT) = Lisbon district** (+ national collections: notarial,
  judicial, passaportes/emigração, overseas-administration personnel, political
  imprisonment). It is the right archive only if an origin resolves to Lisbon;
  otherwise it is the district's Arquivo Distrital. Its passaporte/emigração
  series is a possible lead to a migrant's departure independent of the parish.
- **Azores parish images are free at the GEA** (`culturacores.azores.gov.pt/ig`),
  browsable Ilha → Concelho → Freguesia → Série (not name-indexed); Terceira is
  covered; advanced search is at `arquivos.azores.gov.pt`. Usable only once an
  island + freguesia is fixed from a record.
- Portugal method (confirmed): fix the district from a Brazilian bridge record →
  free DigitArq / FamilySearch → the district's Arquivo Distrital → the diocesan
  or parish archive. Request "certidão em formato narrativo completo" if a paid
  copy is ever needed.
