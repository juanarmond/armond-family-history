# FamilySearch audit and ingestion: Aristão Ferreira Armond

## Objective

Determine whether Aristão Ferreira Armond's authorised FamilySearch Sources or
Memories contain his own 1957 death registration or direct evidence of his
parents, then preserve and catalogue qualifying evidence without relying on
the collaborative tree.

## Scope and access

- Access date: 28 July 2026.
- FamilySearch profile: `GW4P-R9Z`.
- Access method: the repository owner's authorised signed-in browser session.
- FamilySearch was used read-only. No profiles, relationships, sources, tags

## Evidence assessment

The original register provides direct evidence of Aristão's death, its date,
place, age and identity. The death event is `confirmed`.

The two parent names are secondary information reported at death but are
directly stated in the record. Their separate parent-child relationships to
Aristão are therefore `strong-evidence`, not `confirmed`. The record does not
establish that the two reported parents married or partnered, so the family
group contains no partner relationship.

The record does not establish the fuller collaborative forms `Simplício
Ferreira Armond` or `Elizia Balbina Toledo`. Those forms remain leads pending a
birth, baptism or marriage record closer to the parent-child relationships.
