# Aristão Ferreira Armond parish-register access review

## Research question

Can the reported 1879 baptism of Aristão Ferreira Armond, or his marriage to
Liliosa, be located in the original São Sebastião de Leopoldina or Piacatuba
parish registers available through FamilySearch?

## Repositories and collections

- FamilySearch Catalog 345430, *Registros paroquiais, 1852-1924*.
- Image group `004640627`, baptism registers.
- Image group `004640632`, marriage and Piacatuba baptism registers.
- FamilySearch structured index and Full-Text Search.

All searches and viewer checks were performed on 28 July 2026 in the owner's
authenticated FamilySearch session.

## Access result

The opening images for all three items in `004640627`, Item 2 of `004640632`
and Item 4 of `004640632` display `Image Restricted`. The viewer states that
access is determined by local law or the record custodian and does not load a
usable custodian link. The catalog identifies Arquivo Diocesano de Leopoldina
as the original custodian and notes that some images may require access through
a FamilySearch Center or the FamilySearch Library.

The original register pages therefore could not be reviewed in the current
session. This is an access restriction, not a negative search of the registers.
No preview, thumbnail or screenshot was retained as evidence.

## Searchable-layer review

The structured index for image group `004640632` returned no result for:

- `Aristao` with `Armond`;
- `Aristão` with `Armand`;
- `Liliosa` with `Armond`;
- `Aristao` with `Ferreira`; or
- `Liliosa` with `Paz`.

The structured index for image group `004640627` returned no qualifying result
for `Aristao` with `Armond` or `Aristão` with `Armand`. One surname result,
`Antomo Armand- de Nima`, is an unrelated father of Maria and was excluded.

Full-Text Search of image group `004640632`, bounded to 1870–1920, returned:

- no result for `Armond`, `Armand`, `Aristao`, `Aristão`,
  `"Ferreira Armond"`, `"Simplicio Ferreira"`, `"Eliza Ferreira"`,
  `"Liliosa Paz"` or `"Eliza Balbina"`;
- one `Liliosa` result, image ARK `3:1:939N-DS92-64`, which is a false OCR
  match in an unrelated 1920 marriage entry;
- two `Arista*` results, both unrelated;
- fourteen `Simplicio` results, including an unrelated Piacatuba baptism for
  Simplicio Rodrigues de Oliveira; and
- unrelated `Toledo`, `Balbina` and `Elisia` matches.

OCR and index searches are incomplete and do not prove that the target entries
are absent from the restricted images.

## Conclusion

No qualifying baptism or marriage record was found in the searchable layers.
The correct original-image targets have been identified, but both searches are
`inaccessible` until authorised image access is obtained. Aristão's parentage
remains `strong-evidence` from `CIV-0005`; no fuller name form or relationship
was promoted.
