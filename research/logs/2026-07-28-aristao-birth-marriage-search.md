# FamilySearch search: Aristão Ferreira Armond birth and marriage

## Objective

Locate an indexed birth, baptism or marriage record for Aristão Ferreira
Armond, or identify the smallest original-register range that should be
reviewed manually. The target record must be closer to Aristão's birth or
marriage than his 1957 death registration so that the reported names of his
parents can be tested.

## Scope and access

- Access date: 28 July 2026.
- FamilySearch profile used as a navigation lead: `GW4P-R9Z`.
- Access method: the repository owner's authorised signed-in browser session.
- FamilySearch was used read-only. No collaborative profiles, relationships,
  sources, tags or memories were edited.

These are negative index and OCR/full-text searches. They do not establish
that the target record is absent from the original books.

## Parish-register target

FamilySearch catalog entry `345430`, *Registros paroquiais, 1852–1924*, covers
records created by Igreja Católica, São Sebastião, Leopoldina, Minas Gerais,
and held by Arquivo Diocesano de Leopoldina:

- Catalog:
  <https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/345430>
- Baptism image group: `004640627`
- Viewer:
  <https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/film?dgs=004640627&cat=345430&i=233>
- Item 3 opening ARK: `3:1:939J-TWJ6-P`
- Item 3 image range within the image group: 234–497
- Catalogued coverage includes baptism records for 1878–1888, overlapping
  Aristão's reported birth year of 1879.

The catalog metadata associates the group with both Leopoldina and Cataguases
and aggregates several date ranges. The internal title pages and any indexes
must therefore be read before assuming that every image in Item 3 belongs to
one parish or chronological sequence.

## Marriage-series limitation

Image group `004640631` was checked to avoid an unproductive scan:

- Items 3 and 4 are miscellaneous records for August 1869–September 1874 and
  1877–1889, respectively.
- Item 5, images 519–695, is São Sebastião de Leopoldina parish marriage
  volume 2, covering October 1889–July 1897.
- Item 5 opening ARK: `3:1:939N-DS8Y-2`.

If Aristão was born around 1879, that marriage volume ends when he was
approximately eighteen. It is possible but not the most likely series for his
marriage to Liliosa. A later marriage volume, likely beginning after July
1897, should be identified before a manual marriage scan begins.

## Outcome

No qualifying record was located through indexed or full-text search. No
source entity or evidence file was created.

The search did produce a bounded primary-record target: manually review the
1878–1888 baptism sequence in image group `004640627`, beginning with its
internal title pages and indexes and then the 1879 entries. This is more
efficient and more reliable than repeating name-only searches.
