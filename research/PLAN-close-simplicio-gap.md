## The candidate answer (your hypothesis — PLAUSIBLE, not yet proven)
- Blog "Ferreira Armond – Uma Família Mineira" (Além Paraíba): our Simplício = son of
  **Alferes Francisco Ferreira Armond + Felizarda Maria Francisca de Assis Lage**.
- **CAVEAT (why not accept as-is):** that source (a) repeats the debunked France myth,
  (b) says this Simplício died *solteiro/suicide* yet also married w/ children → it merges
  ≥2 Simplícios. Those parents fit the **b.1784 elder Simplício**, not a man whose children
  were born 1873–79. So our Simplício is almost certainly a **later namesake**; his father is
  a **later Francisco Ferreira Armond** in the same branch (likely near the 2º Barão's family).
