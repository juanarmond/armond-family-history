# Full-Text hits: Ferreira Armond in the Muriaé/Leopoldina probate records

## Research question

FamilySearch Full-Text (AI-OCR) search for "Aristão Ferreira Armond" and
"Simplicio Ferreira Armond" in Minas Gerais notarial/probate records.

## Method and provenance

The owner ran the retrieval agent's Full-Text mode; 16 candidate page images were
downloaded and are staged in
`evidence/references/armond-muriae-fulltext-probates/`, with
`fulltext_candidates.csv` listing every hit (query, score, place, date, snippet,
ARK). All are FamilySearch preview images; formal per-source cataloguing to
follow. Everything here is a lead until each image is read and catalogued.

## The decisive hit — 1881 Leopoldina probate/partilha (ARK 3:1:3QHJ-YQWY-R9GH)

A partilha listing heirs, read directly:
- (4º) **Maria Bulandina de Toledo**, casada com Manoel Marques Jorge.
- (5º) **Simplício José Ferreira Armond, casado com D. Eliza Balbina de Toledo.**
- (6º) **Ladisláo Egydio Ferreira de Toledo**, solteiro, 23 anos.
- (7º) **Geraldo Augusto de Toledo Lima**, solteiro, 15 anos, residente na côrte
  (Rio de Janeiro), empregado numa casa de negócio à rua do Visconde de Inhaúma
  nº 74.
- (8º) **Josepha Olympia da Paixão/Conceição**, solteira, 12 anos.
- (plus D. Carolina Leopoldina Marques, casada com Narcizo Marques Braz, já
  falecida, com seus próprios herdeiros.)

This is (very probably) the **estate of Elisa Balbina de Toledo's parent** — the
heirs are the Toledo children, one of whom is **Eliza Balbina de Toledo, wife of
Simplício José Ferreira Armond**.

Significance:
- **The Simplício × Eliza Balbina de Toledo marriage is now documented** in a
  primary court record (1881), independent of the two death records (Aristão
  1957, Marfiza 1962) that also imply it.
- **Elisa's own family is opened:** her siblings (Maria Bulandina de Toledo m.
  Manoel Marques Jorge; Ladisláo Egydio Ferreira de Toledo; Geraldo Augusto de
  Toledo Lima; Josepha Olympia) and her parent (whose 1881 Leopoldina inventário
  this is). Consistent with Nilza Cantoni's lead that Elisa's Toledo family was
  rooted in Argirita.

## Other hits (leads; Simplício and Aristão across the Muriaé/Leopoldina comarcas)

- **Simplício José Ferreira Armond** — recurrently a party/heir, styled
  **"Capitão"**, in probate and property-partition records: Leopoldina 1881,
  1884 (heir of D. Mathilde Maria de Jesus), 1885; Muriaé 1875, 1906, 1907, 1910,
  1913. He was clearly a man of property and standing in this comarca.
- **Aristão Ferreira Armond** — Muriaé probate records 1903 (witness), 1917,
  1919, 1921 (acting in the inventário of Joaquim dos Santos Garcia). Places
  Aristão in the Muriaé area as an adult, ~1903-1921.
- A "Liliosa Paz Armond" hit resolves to the 1973 Rio driver dossier (the
  GOV-0001 record), not new.

## What this establishes / does not

- Establishes (strongly): the Simplício × Elisa marriage; Elisa's Toledo siblings
  and parent's estate; the family's substantial probate/property presence in the
  **Muriaé and Leopoldina comarcas** — which is where the family's
  inventários/testamentos live (the "how wealthy" evidence: Simplício = Capitão).
- Does NOT prove the Barbacena/Azorean bridge — Simplício's own parents are still
  unknown, and nothing links him to the elite Barbacena Armonde family.

## Update 2026-07-30 — the FAN-role images catalogued

(P-0016) or Aristão (P-0008) appear as creditor, witness, appraiser, attorney,
party or co-owner — were catalogued as FAN entities **FAN-0002–FAN-0013**
(alongside FAN-0001, the 1875 procuração), transcribed from the Full-Text OCR
extract, and flattened into `evidence/references/` under `FAN-NNNN-…` names. The
`armond-muriae-fulltext-probates/` subfolder and its set README were removed; the
full candidate list was preserved as
`logs/2026-07-29-fulltext-muriae-leopoldina-candidates.csv`. The two subject
records remain catalogued as PRB-0001 (1881 inventário) and PRB-0002 (1884
petition) in `evidence/probate/`.
