# 2026-09-25 — Cágado (→ Mar de Espanha) 1831 population map: bounded negative for Armond

Written up 2026-10-08 from the P-0016 note in `data/record-coverage.yaml` (commit e1f6c82),
which until then was the only record of this search. No new reading was made.

## Research question

Does any Armond, in any spelling, appear in the 1831 population map of the Curato das Mercês
do Cágado — the later Mar de Espanha, which held the district of Bom Jesus do Rio Pardo from
July 1863 to November 1869 (C-002), where the Simplício × Eliza marriage (P-0016) may have
been registered?

## Repository and collection

Arquivo Público Mineiro (APM), Mapas de População: "Relação dos Habitantes do Curato da
Capella das Mercês do Kag.do, Termo da Villa de Barbacena", signed Francisco Leite Ribeiro
at Kágado on 1 October 1831. 19 page images, delivered in the retrieval drop.

- Archive reference reads approximately Cx 07 / Doc 07 but is **unverified**: the source
  URL was never captured.
- The APM catalogues the map under 1834, not 1831. Nilza Cantoni traced the Roza Maria lead
  to this digitisation coding error.
- Cágado was a curato under São João Nepomuceno, with no register series of its own before
  1851. The Arraial de N.S. das Mercês do Cágado became Mar de Espanha in 1851.

## Names, variants, places and dates

Armond, Armonde, Ermonde, Asmonde, Ormonde. Also Toledo, Paz, Assis, Lage, Lima and
Rodrigues do Valle. Place: Cágado, termo de Barbacena. Date: 1831.

## Reads

- **2026-09-19:** first read, to close the Roza Maria lead (not found; see the local triage
  ledger, 2026-09-19/22).
- **2026-09-24:** re-read fogo by fogo.

## Results and negative results

About 88 fogos (about 1,110 people, about 60% of them enslaved). **No Armond, Armonde,
Ermonde, Asmonde or Ormonde in any spelling.** Toledo, Paz, Assis, Lage, Lima and Rodrigues
do Valle are also absent.

## Bounds — read before citing the negative

- The document has **no closing recapitulation table**, and the list stops mid-household at
  fogo 88. The corpus is therefore probably **incomplete**: either unscanned sheets follow
  or the summary sits on an unphotographed verso. The negative covers the 88 fogos actually
  read, **not the district**.
- Column 4 of the right-hand page was cropped out of images p12–p15, so condição and age are
  missing for many entries in fogos 33–61. Names and qualidade were legible throughout, so
  the **surname** negative holds.
- **Variants (bound added 2026-10-08):** only R-forms were searched. The retrieval side
  adopted a 44-variant standard with L-forms (Almond-, Almend-, …) on 2026-09-25, after a
  32-variant sweep missed "Almondes" in the 1831 Meia Pataca map. The Cágado images are
  deleted, so this cannot be re-swept, and it stays an **R-form negative**.

## Access restrictions and retention

The 19 images were deleted from the retrieval drop on 2026-09-24 under its keep-only-family
rule. No image is held in this repository, so the map is not catalogued as a source. The
fogo-by-fogo transcription existed only in the gitignored drop. On 2026-10-08 it was no
longer there, and no copy was found elsewhere on the working machine, so it is lost. This
log and the P-0016 coverage note are the durable record. If the images are obtained again,
catalogue them as a negative-evidence government source on the GOV-0012 pattern
(`evidence_type: negative`, `usage: context_only`).

## Conclusions

Bounded negative for Armond within the 88 fogos read. It does not exclude the Cágado
district, and it says nothing about Bom Jesus do Rio Pardo itself.
