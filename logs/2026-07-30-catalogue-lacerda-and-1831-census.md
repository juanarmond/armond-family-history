# 2026-07-30 — Catalogue the Lacerda 2010 thesis and the 1831 Curral Novo census

## Objective

Promote two long-standing thesis-mining leads from the retrieval-agent
resources cache into catalogued sources, through the value gate: the Lacerda
2010 UFF doctoral thesis and the 1831 Curral Novo (Barbacena) population list.

## Method and provenance

Both files were already present in `research/from-retrieval/resources/`
(retrieved 2026-07-29). Each was read against its original before cataloguing,
never from the retrieval agent's synthesis notes:

- **Lacerda**: title page and ficha catalográfica verified — Antônio Henrique
  Duarte Lacerda, "Negócios de Minas ... a família Ferreira Armonde (1751-1850)",
  Tese de Doutorado, UFF, Niterói, abril de 2010 (2 vols.; advisor Carlos Gabriel
  Guimarães; bibliografia f. 486-504).
- **1831 census**: read in full. Correction to the agent's note — the Ferreira
  Armonde household is the **fifth quarteirão's fogo 14**, not "fogo 14".

## Result

- **PUB-0002** (published_genealogy, source_form authored_narrative, lead_only):
  Lacerda's document-based history of the Barbacena Ferreira Armonde family
  (1751-1850); cited by Chagas 2018 (PUB-0001) for the Azorean-origin claim.
- **GOV-0002** (census, source_form derivative, context_only): the Projeto
  Compartilhar transcription of the APM manuscript. Quinto Quarteirão, fogo 14 =
  widow Felizarda Maria Francisca de Assis (70) with six unmarried adult sons,
  incl. **Simplício José Ferreira (46, solteiro)**, plus 66 cativos; the list is
  dated 23/09/1831 and signed by Simplício José Ferreira Armond, Juiz de Paz.
- Evidence PDFs preserved in `evidence/publications/` and `evidence/government/`;
  inventory `DOC-0022` and `DOC-0023`; both linked to P-0016 (Simplício).

## Significance

The anti-merge now rests on catalogued sources rather than an un-catalogued
cache: the Barbacena Gen-2 Simplício (b.~1785, celibate in 1831) is not this
repository's Simplício José Ferreira Armond (P-0016), husband of Eliza Balbina
de Toledo and father of Aristão. Both sources remain **context/leads** about the
collateral Barbacena family; neither evidences descent, and the
Piacatuba-to-Barbacena bridge is still unproven.
