# Liliosa Paz Armond death-record search

## Research question

Can either reported 1946 death date for Liliosa Paz Armond be supported by her
own civil death registration or parish burial record?

## Scope and method

The authenticated FamilySearch historical-record index was searched on 28 July
2026 using exact and variant forms including:

- `Liliosa Paz Armond`;
- `Liliosa Armond`;
- `Liliosa Ferreira Armond`;
- `Liliosa Paz`;
- exact and fuzzy given-name, surname and spouse combinations; and
- 1946, 1945–1947, Brazil, Rio de Janeiro and Minas Gerais constraints.

Open-web exact-name searches and FamilySearch full-text searches were also
reviewed. Candidate images were inspected rather than accepted from OCR.

## Indexed and full-text results

No qualifying civil death or parish burial record was returned.

Exact-given-name searches returned unrelated people including Liliosa Maes de
França (`1:1:7M1H-FCZM`), Liliosa Clara dos Santos (`1:1:WFWV-5K6Z`) and
Liliosa Cavalcanti dos Santos. Fuzzy searches produced unrelated Armond, Paz
and spouse-name results.

The record `1:1:6VJG-SQ1N`, indexed with the malformed name "Liliosa Saz
Armonde", is a mention in Sonia Maria Armond de Oliveira's birth record. It is
not Liliosa's own death record.

An exact full-text name hit led to a March 1973 Guanabara government
driver-dossier index, not a death record. The original image directly names
José Olavo Armond's mother as `LILIOSA PAZ ARMOND` and prints his father as
`ARISTAC FERREIRA ARMOND`. That separate evidence is catalogued as `GOV-0001`;
the apparent corruption `Aristac` is preserved rather than silently
normalised.

## Bounded register review

FamilySearch Images identified image group `004366685`, item 1, titled
`Registros de óbito: Volta Redonda. Death Records November 1946–May 1947`.
The series begins in November 1946 and can therefore test the reported
15 November date in Volta Redonda, but not the reported 16 April date.

Original images 8–11 were reviewed. They cover registrations from 14 through
20 November and deaths around the reported 15 November date:

- image 8, ARK `3:1:S3HT-DY69-2FQ`;
- image 9, ARK `3:1:S3HT-DY69-R72`;
- image 10, ARK `3:1:S3HT-DY69-JCK`; and
- image 11, ARK `3:1:S3HT-DY69-3X1`.

No entry for Liliosa, Aristão or an identifiable Armond household appeared in
that bounded window. The images contained unrelated records and were not
retained.

The FamilySearch Images search exposed no Volta Redonda district death image
group covering January through October 1946 under the historical place and
death filters. A review of the 1946 place-index results also produced no
explicit 16 April match.

## Conclusion

Liliosa's own death or burial record was not located.

The 15 November 1946 lead is not supported by the accessible immediate
Volta Redonda registration window. This is bounded negative evidence, not proof
that she did not die on that date or elsewhere.

The 16 April 1946 lead remains untested in an original register because the
accessible Volta Redonda series begins in November. Neither date is promoted,
and both remain preserved as unresolved leads.

## Next research action

Identify the source that introduced each 1946 date and the civil or parish
jurisdiction in which Liliosa was living. Search April 1946 death and burial
books for that jurisdiction before extending the manual review to neighbouring
municipalities.
