## PRB-0008 — José Cesário de Toledo Lima × Claudina Brandina de Jesus inventário, 1867

**Record:** Inventário, Concórdia (Districto do Rio Pardo, MG); opened 4 March 1867;
archive reference [38403 268]; J. de Bastos Pacheco, Escrivão dos Órfãos; Judge:
Dr. Mortimário de Sousa Lintz.

**gen1 (cover):** Explicitly names both deceased spouses: "INVENTÁRIO / José Cesario
de Tolledo Lima / Claudina Brandina de Jesus." This is the **first primary record to
state Claudina's full name** — "Claudina Brandina de Jesus" — primary-confirming what
had previously been inferred from her daughter's name variant.

**p8 (children list):** Items 4 through 9 visible. Item 6: "Simplicio José Ferrara
[Ferreira] de Tolledo, Solteiro de quatorze annos." Items 7–9: Ladisláo (12), Geraldo
(2), unnamed female infant (6 months). Item 5 is on the unread p7. Certification dated
7 March 1867, Concórdia.

**p9 (curator oath):** Aureliano de Sales Toledo (JC's sibling, item 5 of Ladisláo's
children) took the curator oath before Judge Mortimário de Sousa Lintz. Two certifying
signatures: "Je de Bastos Paz" and "J. de Freitas Pacheco" (likely same person,
clerk's name uncertain).

## Critical data correction: Eliza is NOT JC's daughter

**The error:** The previous reading of PRB-0006 folio 9 — "5º Simplicio José Ferreira
Armond, cazado com D. Eliza Balbina de Tolledo" — was interpreted as listing Eliza as
JC's fifth child/representante. This was wrong. The text places **Simplício** as heir
#5 and names **Eliza as his wife**. A married woman's inheritance was administered
through her husband, hence the phrasing "Simplicio … cazado com D. Eliza."

**The evidence:**
- PRB-0008-p8: JC's own inventário lists his children; Eliza does not appear.
  Simplício is item 6.
- PRB-0009-p2: Ladisláo's inventário lists JC's representantes; Eliza does not
  appear. Simplício is item 6.

**Consequence:** Eliza Balbina de Toledo's parentage is now **unresolved**. She bears
the Toledo surname, is connected to the family through her marriage to Simplício (JC's
son), and could be a daughter of any of Ladisláo's other children. The direct line is
Ladisláo → JC → Simplício.
