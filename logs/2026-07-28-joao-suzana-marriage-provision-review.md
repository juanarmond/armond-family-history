# João Monis Bittencourt and Susanna Rita Brondão marriage-provision review

## Research question

Does the collaborative-tree date of 23 December 1882 represent the couple's
marriage, and what original record supports it?

## Profiles and attached material reviewed

- João Muniz Bittencourt, FamilySearch profile `GLMF-KCR`: four attached
  sources and no Memories.
- Suzana Ritta Brandão, FamilySearch profile `LB1N-YC9`: three attached sources
  and no Memories.

The profiles are navigation leads only. Their 1864 and 1868 birth years,
reported São Miguel origin, proposed parents and later historical-record
attachments were not promoted.

## Interpretation correction

FamilySearch describes the item as a marriage and its citation gives
23 December 1882. The original handwriting is more specific: provisions were
issued on that date so that João Monis Bittencourt and Susanna Rita Brondão
could marry in the mother church of the Espírito Santo parish.

The entry therefore directly proves the provision, intended couple, date and
parish. It does not prove that the ceremony occurred on 23 December or that it
occurred at all. The repository models an `other` event for the provision and
retains the partnership at `strong-evidence`; it does not create a confirmed
marriage event.

The FamilySearch detail table currently displays the year `1633`, which
conflicts with both the original image and the record citation. The malformed
index value is not used.

## Other source risks

The João profile attaches an external Hemeroteca link labelled 1871
naturalisation and asserts an 1864 São Miguel birth. The external page was
unavailable during this review, and the chronology would require careful
identity analysis rather than automatic attachment.

A 1950 Espírito Santo civil record and additional Rio de Janeiro civil-record
leads are also attached to the profiles. They were not used to establish the
1882 couple and require person-by-person image review.
