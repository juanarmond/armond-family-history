## Transcription findings

The safely legible portions identify:

- Antenor Muniz and Iris Bohrer as the spouses;
- marriage at Alvorada, Minas Gerais, on 7 December 1949;
- Antenor as single and a `lavrador`, translated in the abstract as farm
  worker;
- Antenor's parents as Deocleciano Muniz and Luiza Fernandes de Azevedo;
- Iris's parents as João Gonçalves Bohrer and Selina Bohrer;
- the married-name form `Iris Bohrer Muniz`; and
- an observation reading `Nada consta`.

The certificate appears to have been issued at Alvorada on 11 August 1955.
Book, page, record number and several vital-detail fields are obscured by
folds, tape, stains or low contrast and are not supplied from the collaborative
profiles or earlier assistant transcriptions.

## Evidence assessment

The certificate gives direct evidence of the marriage, the spouse names,
Antenor's occupation and Iris's married-name form. The reported parents are
secondary information but are directly stated. Because the certificate is a
derivative of the register entry and the retained file is reconstructed from
viewer tiles, the marriage, partner relationship and parent-child edges are
classified `strong-evidence`, not `confirmed`.

The record names two parents for Iris but does not prove a relationship between
those parents. They are therefore represented with `parent` member roles and
separate parent-child edges, without a `partner_relationship`.
