## Conclusions

- **New people (P-0139–P-0150) and families (F-0070–F-0075):**
  - Manoel João Fernandes da Cruz × Anna Maria, a foundling;
  - Bento José Fernandes × Joaquina Rosa Lopes;
  - Manoel Fernandes × Jozefa Maria Domingues;
  - Ignacio Francisco Lopes × Marianna da Silva;
  - Constantino Fernandes × Roza Maria;
  - André Domingues × Maria Luiza.
  These are 3×- to 6×-great-grandparents of the owner's maternal grandfather, Antenor Muniz.
- **Statuses:**
  - Confirmed: Manoel João's parentage (his own baptism, marriage, and his mother's procuração); the couples
    F-0070 and F-0071.
  - Strong evidence: Thereza (P-0039) as the testator's daughter, which rests on name, husband and district;
    Bento's 1807 baptism and 1860 death, because of the age conflict; and the generations above Bento.
  - Hypothesis: the 1771 baptism.
- **Documented children:**
  - F-0070: Thereza's eight siblings. The four who survived match the will's "nove filhos, achando-se vivos
    presentemente apenas cinco" exactly, together with Thereza.
  - F-0071: Manoel João's eight siblings. Two are sons named José: the first, born 1839, died in 1845, so the José
    who married in 1868 is a second son.
  - F-0072 and F-0075: two aunts and one aunt respectively.
- **Corrections:**
  - Thereza's origin is the Minho. The profile statements "born in the Zona da Mata", "parents unknown" and
    "landless" are superseded on P-0039, P-0038 and P-0013 and in the family story.
  - Thereza could not write (PRB-0014).
  - "Secundino de Azevedo Maia", in the clerk's 1898 copy of the will, is most probably contamination from the
    witness Domingos Affonso de Azevedo Maia. It is not recorded as a name variant.
  - Leonidia Fernandes de Azevedo is not Secundino's daughter (retrieval-side reading of COARPE 38410248).
- **Liliosa (P-0009):**
  - Bounded negative: the 1897 Muriaé "Paz" inventário is D. Olympia de Souza Paz's. Her seven heirs exclude
    Liliosa.
  - Two Paz/Paes households remain as leads.
  - The Rio birth registration of a granddaughter, indexed with Aristão × Liliosa as maternal grandparents, is
    access-restricted.

## Open conflicts and leads recorded, not resolved

- **Manoel Fernandes's death.** An 1839 baptism names a living "Manoel Fernandes avo materno" of Quintão, yet an
  1837 baptism calls Bento's parents "ja defuntos". The 1839 man may be a namesake (see P-0143).
- **Anna Maria's name.** She is "Joanna Maria" at her 1855 marriage and "Anna" in every later record.
- **Bento's age.** He is aged 60 at the 1860 death, against a birth in 1807.
- **Two identifications.** Joaquim (born 1866) as Joaquim José Fernandes, and Maria (born 1871) as Maria Fernandes de
  Souza, are inferences.
- **Unheld lead.** An 1890 Ucha entry names a Thereza de Jezus Fernandes. Its first page (DGS 005252910 image 15) is
  not held.
